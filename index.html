GOOP VR BUILDER

Build and edit entirely inside immersive VR. The menu follows your left wrist, stays angled toward your headset, and can be hidden or reopened with the three-line wrist button. With Quest hand tracking, pinch parts to grab and move them; controllers also work as a fallback.

Checking WebXR…

In-headset controls Left hand: wrist-mounted editor menu; the three-line button at its upper-left hides or reopens it Hands: point with your right index finger and pinch to press a menu button Hands: select a part to show its XYZ gizmo; pinch a double-ended arrow and push/pull to move on that axis Hands: pinch the wide colored ring and turn your hand around it to rotate without moving the part's center Snap Settings: toggle position/rotation snapping and enter custom grid distance and angle steps Hands: pinch a part itself to freely grab and move it Controllers: left thumbstick walks/strafe the player relative to headset direction; right thumbstick left/right snap-turns the player by 30° Controllers: point + trigger to select; use the enlarged XYZ arrow hit areas and wide rotation rings while holding grip to manipulate parts
