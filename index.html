Build and edit entirely inside immersive VR. The menu follows your left wrist. With Quest hand tracking, pinch parts to grab and move them; controllers also work as a fallback.

In-headset controls Left hand: wrist-mounted editor menu Hands: point with your right index finger and pinch to press a menu button Hands: select a part to show its XYZ gizmo; pinch a double-ended arrow and push/pull to move on that axis Hands: pinch a colored circle and turn your hand around it to rotate without moving the part's center Snap Settings: toggle position/rotation snapping and enter custom grid distance and angle steps Hands: pinch a part itself to freely grab and move it Controllers: point + trigger; aim at an XYZ arrow or rotation ring and hold grip to manipulate it
